Our flour

The rye, spelt and emmer are stone-ground at a family mill in Sweden and come to us in 25 kg sacks.

We mix the doughs the evening before and start baking at half past six, so the first loaves are cooling when we open.

Book a table

Six of our tables can be booked, for up to 8 people and 90 minutes. The rest are kept for walk-ins.
