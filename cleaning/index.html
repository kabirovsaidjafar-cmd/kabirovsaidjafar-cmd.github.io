Who comes to your home

Ana Mendes started cleaning houses on her own in 2014. There are eight of us now, working in pairs, and you get the same pair every visit.

Everyone is on our payroll and covered by our insurance. We’re paid by the hour, not by the house, so nobody rushes your home to get to the next one.

Questions people ask

Do I need to be home?
No. Most people leave us a key or a door code. Keys go in a locked box with a number on the tag, never your address.
What about pets?
Tell us their names when you book. We’re happy to work around them and keep doors shut if you ask.
Do you bring supplies?
Yes: cloths, mops, a vacuum and plant-based cleaning products. If you’d like us to use your own, leave them out.
What if something gets broken?
Tell us the same day. We’re insured, and we’ll repair or replace it.
What if I’m not happy with a clean?
Call us within 24 hours and we’ll come back and do it again, free.
Which areas do you cover?
Riverside, Mill Park, Eastbank, Old Town and North Hill. Somewhere close by? Ask.
