Today’s emergency appointments

 

Call us straight away if you have

  • a tooth knocked out (keep it in milk and come in within the hour)
  • swelling in your face or jaw
  • pain that painkillers don’t help
  • bleeding from your mouth that won’t stop

If your face is swelling and you find it hard to breathe or swallow, call 911.
