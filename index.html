I build websites for small businesses.

Here are four, for a renovation firm, a bakery, a dentist and a cleaning company. The businesses are made up; the websites are real, and everything on them works.

Each one is built from scratch, works on phones and keeps its text, prices and photos in one file, so changes are quick.

  1. 01

    Varga Renovation

    Kitchens, bathrooms and additions

    Open the site

    The Varga Renovation website: a large headline promising the price in writing, above a photo of an oak kitchen island

    • A real multi-page site: services, finished jobs, about and contact, thirteen pages in all, with one menu and footer.
    • Each service has a ballpark that shows a price range and the weeks. The choice carries over to the quote form.
    • Every service and every finished job has a weeks strip that shows what happens when.
  2. 02

    Hedvig

    Swedish bakery and coffee bar

    Open the site

    The Hedvig website: today’s oven board with bake times beside a photo of sockerkaka

    • The first screen is today’s oven board: what’s out, what’s next, and a photo of each bake.
    • Opening hours, the board and table booking all follow the bakery’s own clock.
    • Smooth scrolling, with animations that play again when you scroll back up.
  3. 03

    Haddad Dental

    Family dentist

    Open the site

    The Haddad Dental website: a side card with the next free check-up beside a photo of the dentist explaining an X-ray

    • A side card always shows whether the practice is open and the next free check-up.
    • Today’s emergency appointments, and a plain list of when to call straight away.
    • Booking asks one thing at a time: what you need, the day, the time, your details.
  4. 04

    Mendes Cleaning

    House cleaning company

    Open the site

    The Mendes Cleaning website: the headline is a price sentence with drop-downs for home size and how often

    • The headline is the price. Change the home size or how often, and it updates.
    • What gets cleaned, room by room, scrolls sideways on a computer.
    • The price table and the booking form keep the same choice.

Built with Next.js, React and TypeScript. Photos from Unsplash, credited on each site.
