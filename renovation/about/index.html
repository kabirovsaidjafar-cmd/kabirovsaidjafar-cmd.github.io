About

A family firm, nine people.

Tibor Varga started the firm in 2009 from his garage, making kitchens for neighbors. His daughter Elena joined in 2013 and now runs the quotes and the schedule. We have stayed small on purpose: Elena visits every house herself and reads every quote before it goes out.

How it started

One bathroom, finished in a weekend

We took the first bathroom job because a neighbor’s plumber disappeared halfway through. Tibor finished it in a weekend, and the neighbor told three other people. That is still most of how we find work.

Today there are nine of us. Five work on the tools, one runs the sites and one does the books. Plumbing, electrical and gas work is done by licensed local firms we have used for years. Their names are on your quote.
