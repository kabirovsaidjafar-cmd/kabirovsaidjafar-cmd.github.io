How a job runs

Five steps, always in this order

You pay for work you have seen, and you hear about every change before it happens.

  1. 01

    A site visit

    Elena comes to the house, usually for an hour. She measures, opens what she can open and asks what bothers you about the room. It costs nothing.

  2. 02

    A written price

    Within five working days you get one number, with a list of what is in it and what is not. The price holds for 60 days.

  3. 03

    A start date

    You pay 10% to book the start. After that you pay at three points written on the quote, each tied to work you can see.

  4. 04

    The work, with a Friday update

    Every Friday you get photos and a text: what was done, what is next week and whether anything has changed. The price changes only if you change the job, and only after you sign the change.

  5. 05

    A walk-through

    We go around the finished room with you and write a punch list. We fix everything on it within 14 days. The last payment is due after the walk-through.

Our rules

Four rules on every site

Site rulesVarga Renovation

  1. One price, in writing

    The quote says what is in and what is out. It changes only if you change the job, and only after you have signed the change.

  2. One person to call

    Your project lead’s cell number is on the first page of the quote. She answers it.

  3. A clean site

    Floors covered, dust sheets up and the house swept every evening. The dumpster is emptied before it overflows.

  4. Bad news the same day

    If we find something behind a wall, you hear about it before we leave that day, with a price for dealing with it.
