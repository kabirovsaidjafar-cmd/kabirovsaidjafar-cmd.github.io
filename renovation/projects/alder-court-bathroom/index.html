Bath and shower room, Alder Court

A small en suite grew into a bathroom with a freestanding tub, once the closet next to it gave up its space.

Where
Alder Court, Riverside
Finished
August 2025
Size
85 sq ft
Cost
$24,600
On site
4 weeks

The brief

The en suite was a shower stall and a toilet. The closet next door held towels. The owners wanted a proper tub and agreed to give up the closet to get one.

What we did

We took the closet wall out and moved the toilet. A freestanding tub now sits under the window, with a sink on a long white vanity. The shower is in the corner, behind a glass door with a black frame.

Week by week

The 4 weeks

Week1234
  1. Demolition and wallWeek 1
  2. Pipes, electrical, floorWeeks 1 to 2
  3. Waterproofing and testWeek 2
  4. TileWeek 3
  5. Fixtures, glass, paintWeek 4

What went differently

The freestanding tub was backordered by three weeks. We built the room around a temporary frame of the same size, so tile and pipework kept going. The tub went in on day 22.

Materials

  • Freestanding acrylic tub

  • Black-framed glass door
  • Gray floor tile
