Rear addition and attic room, Cedar Road

A long kitchen with yard doors on the ground floor, and a bedroom and study in the roof.

Where
Cedar Road, Riverside
Finished
June 2026
Size
320 sq ft addition, 180 sq ft attic
Cost
$118,000
On site
16 weeks

The brief

A three-bedroom house with a kitchen that faced a wall and an attic full of boxes. The owners wanted a kitchen that opened onto the yard and two more rooms upstairs, without moving out.

What we did

We built a one-story addition across the back, with cabinets along one wall and glass doors at the end. In the roof we added a bedroom with a skylight and a study under the exposed beams. A new staircase rises from the landing.

What went differently

Rain in week 4 flooded the foundation trench. We pumped it out and waited two days before pouring. We made up the time by starting the attic structure while the walls of the addition were curing.
