Family bathroom, Harbor Road

A cramped bathroom with a boxed-in tub became a long room with a walk-in shower and space to turn around.

Where
Harbor Road, Riverside
Finished
January 2026
Size
60 sq ft
Cost
$18,900
On site
3 weeks

The brief

The tub was boxed in with a drywall shelf, and the shower over it was a curtain. The family wanted a walk-in shower and a tub they would actually use, in the same footprint.

What we did

We took out the shelf, moved the sink to the long wall and built a walk-in shower with a fixed glass screen. Floor and walls are large stone-effect tile. The vanity is oak with two drawers, wide enough for two toothbrushes and a basket of towels.

Week by week

The 3 weeks

Week123
  1. Demolition and pipesWeek 1
  2. Waterproofing and testWeeks 1 to 2
  3. TileWeek 2
  4. Fixtures, glass, paintWeek 3

What went differently

The water test showed a slow drop in the shower pan. A fitting under the pan had not been tightened. Fixing it took an hour, and it meant no leak behind the tile.
