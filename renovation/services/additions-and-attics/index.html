Additions and attic rooms

These are the jobs with the longest list: drawings, a building permit, foundations or a new roof structure, then the finishing. We handle the paperwork and the structural engineer, and we send a photo update every Friday so you can follow the job without standing in the yard.

What is included

  • Drawings and the building permit application
  • The structural engineer’s calculations
  • Foundations or roof work, walls, windows and doors
  • Insulation, heating, electrical and drywall
  • Flooring, paint and a finished room
  • Building inspector visits arranged and attended

What is not

  • Permit and engineer fees, which are listed on the quote at cost
  • A new kitchen or bathroom inside the addition (priced as its own job)
  • Landscaping beyond putting the yard back

Questions

What people ask us

Do I need a permit?

Yes, for work like this. We draw the plans, apply for the building permit and book the inspections. The permit fee is on the quote at what the town charges, with no mark-up.

Can we live in the house during the build?

Almost always. The old back wall stays up until the new structure is weathertight, so the house stays warm and secure. You will have noise and dust, and you will lose the yard for a few months.

What if the ground is worse than the drawings assume?

We dig a trial hole at the visit, which is the best check we have. If the foundations need to go deeper than planned, the engineer decides, we tell you the same day and you sign the extra cost before we dig.

Tell us about the room.

The site visit is free and takes about an hour. You get the written price within five working days.
