Bathrooms

A bathroom is small, so every mistake shows. We waterproof the whole wet area, fill it and leave it for 24 hours before tiling, and one tiler does the room from the first tile to the last so the lines stay straight.

What is included

  • Taking out the old fixtures and hauling them away
  • New pipework and a new drain where the layout changes
  • Waterproofing and a 24-hour water test
  • Wall and floor tile, grouted and sealed
  • Tub, shower, sink, toilet, vanity and mirror installed
  • A heated towel warmer or heated floor, if you choose one

What is not

  • The fixtures and tile themselves, unless you ask us to order them
  • Moving a main drain through a structural wall
  • Repainting other rooms
A medium job

Questions

What people ask us

Will we have a working bathroom?

Not for the weeks the room is open. If it is the only bathroom in the house, tell us at the visit. We then plan the order so the toilet works on most days, and we can rent a portable unit.

What does the water test involve?

After the waterproofing is on, we fill the shower pan or wet area, mark the level and leave it for 24 hours. If the level has dropped, we find out before it is behind tile.

Can you install a bathroom suite I bought myself?

Yes. We check it at the visit, because a missing part is the usual reason a job stops for a week.

Tell us about the room.

The site visit is free and takes about an hour. You get the written price within five working days.
