Kitchens

The old kitchen hides things: a pipe in the wrong place, a floor that is not level, a wall that is not straight. At the site visit we open up what we can reach and measure the rest twice. If something changes the job after that, you hear the same day, with a price.

From
$14,000
Takes
4 to 12 weeks
Price
Fixed, in writing

What is included

  • Taking out the old kitchen and hauling it away
  • New plumbing and electrical where the layout changes
  • Drywall, plaster, a new floor and paint
  • Cabinets, countertops, sink and backsplash, installed and leveled
  • Appliances connected and tested
  • A punch-list walk-through, with fixes within 14 days

What is not

  • The appliances themselves (we can order them at trade price)
  • Curtains, blinds and loose furniture
  • Structural steel for a wall removal, which is priced once an engineer has drawn it

Questions

What people ask us

Can we use the kitchen while you work?

No. For about six weeks you will cook somewhere else. Most people set up a kettle, a microwave and a table in another room. If you have a laundry room with a sink, we can leave that connected.

Do you supply the cabinets?

We can, or you can buy them and we install them. Installing cabinets you bought costs less, but the cabinets are then your warranty to sort out with the shop, not ours.

How long until you can start?

About six weeks from signing the quote. A quote holds its price for 60 days.

Tell us about the room.

The site visit is free and takes about an hour. You get the written price within five working days.
